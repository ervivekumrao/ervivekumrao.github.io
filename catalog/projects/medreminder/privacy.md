# Privacy Policy for Med Reminder

*Last updated: June 25, 2026*

Med Reminder is designed to help you manage your medications while keeping your personal health data private and secure.

## 1. Local Health Data
Your medication schedules, dosages, and adherence history are stored locally on your device using a secure database.

## 2. Permissions
- **Camera & OCR:** Used to scan medication labels on-device via Google ML Kit. No images are stored or transmitted.
- **Notifications:** Essential for displaying timely medication reminders.
- **Exact Alarms:** Used to ensure reminders trigger at precise times.

## 3. Zero External Transmission
Personal health information, medication lists, and schedules are never transmitted to external servers.
