# Privacy Policy for Med Reminder

*Last Updated: June 25, 2026*

Welcome to **Med Reminder**. We understand that your health information is deeply personal. This application is designed to help you manage your medications while keeping your data private and secure.

## 1. Information We Access
To provide a functional and reliable reminder service, the app requires access to several device features:

- **Local Health Data:** Your medication schedules, dosages, and adherence history are stored **locally** on your device using a secure database.
- **Camera & OCR:** The app uses your device's **Camera** to scan medication labels on-device via Google ML Kit. No images are stored or transmitted; the feed is processed in real-time.
- **Notifications & Alarms:** Used to display reminders on your lock screen at exact scheduled times.

## 2. Data Privacy & Local-First Approach
- **No Server Transmission:** Personal health information and medication lists are **never** transmitted to external servers.
- **No Marketing:** We never sell your health data or share it with third-party advertisers.

## 3. User Control & Security
You have full authority to edit or permanently delete any medication entry or user profile at any time.
