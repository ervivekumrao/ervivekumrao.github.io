# Privacy Policy for Career Crox Calling Assistant

*Last updated: June 25, 2026*

Career Crox Calling Assistant synchronizes candidate call queues with the enterprise Career Crox CRM.

## 1. Information Sync
Call logs, call outcomes, resumes, and optional call audio recordings are uploaded to your organization's Career Crox CRM instance for recruitment auditing.

## 2. Secure Pairing
Device communication is authenticated via 6-digit session codes and encrypted enterprise tokens.
