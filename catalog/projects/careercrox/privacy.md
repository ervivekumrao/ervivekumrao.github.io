# Privacy Policy for Career Crox – Calling Assistant

*Last Updated: June 25, 2026*

Welcome to **Career Crox – Calling Assistant**. This application is designed to help recruitment professionals manage and track candidate interactions securely.

## 1. Sensitive Data Access Disclosure
- **Call Outcome Sync:** The app reads call logs (`READ_CALL_LOG`) to identify recruitment calls and sync details (timestamp, duration, status) with your paired Career Crox CRM.
- **Caller Identification:** Access to phone state (`READ_PHONE_STATE`) identifies incoming/outgoing calls from candidates in your recruitment queue.
- **Contact Matching:** Contact access matches CRM candidate profiles with your local contact list for caller identification.

## 2. Data Transmission & Security
- **Secure CRM Pairing:** Data is only transmitted to your authorized Career Crox CRM instance paired via a 6-digit code.
- **End-to-End Encryption:** All data sent to your CRM is encrypted.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell or share call logs or candidate data.
