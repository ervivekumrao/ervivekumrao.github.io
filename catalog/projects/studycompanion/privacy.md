# Privacy Policy for Study Companion

*Last updated: June 25, 2026*

Welcome to **Study Companion**. We believe in empowering students through technology while maintaining a safe and private learning environment. This policy explains how we handle your educational data.

## 1. Information We Access
To provide AI-driven learning features, the app requires access to several device features:
- **Local Learning Data:** All student profiles, scanned textbook images, and generated study materials are stored **locally** on your device using a Room database.
- **Camera & OCR:** The app uses your device's Camera to scan textbooks and handwritten notes using Google ML Kit on-device OCR.
- **AI Question Generation:** When you generate practice quizzes, extracted text is processed by AI services (such as Gemini or Google ML Kit). This processing is used strictly to generate educational content.
- **Storage:** Required to save generated flashcards, study streaks, and progress reports locally on your device.

## 2. Data Privacy & Accountability
- **No Account Required:** You can use all features of Study Companion without creating an account or providing personal identifying information.
- **No Data Sharing:** We do not sell or share your scanned notes or study patterns with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

## 3. User Control
- You can delete any student profile or study set permanently at any time from within the app.
- Clearing app cache or data in system settings will remove all locally stored educational materials.
