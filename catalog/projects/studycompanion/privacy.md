# Privacy Policy for Study Companion

*Last Updated: June 25, 2026*

Welcome to **Study Companion**. We believe in empowering students through technology while maintaining a safe and private learning environment.

## 1. Information We Access
- **Local Learning Data:** All student profiles, scanned textbook images, and generated study materials are stored **locally** on your device.
- **Camera & OCR:** Optical Character Recognition is processed on-device via Google ML Kit to extract text from your notes.
- **AI Question Generation:** Text is processed strictly to generate educational content and is not used to build personal profiles.

## 2. Data Privacy & Accountability
- **No Account Required:** You can use all features without creating an account or providing personal identifying information.
- **No Data Sharing:** Scanned notes and study patterns are never sold or shared with third parties.

## 3. User Control
You can delete any student profile or study set permanently at any time within the app.
