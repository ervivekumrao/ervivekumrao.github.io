# Privacy Policy for Study Companion

*Last updated: June 25, 2026*

Study Companion empowers learning through AI while maintaining a private student environment.

## 1. Local Learning Data
All student profiles, scanned textbook images, and generated study materials are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Permissions
- **Camera & OCR:** Used to scan textbooks and handwritten notes using Google ML Kit OCR on-device.
- **Storage:** Required to save generated flashcards, study streaks, and progress reports locally.

## 3. No Account Required
You can use all features without creating an account or providing personal identifying information.

## 4. Contact Us
For support or questions, please contact `manuscriptcode@gmail.com`.
