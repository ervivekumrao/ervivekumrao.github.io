# Privacy Policy for App Manager

*Last updated: June 25, 2026*

Welcome to **App Manager**. This application is an enterprise-grade system utility designed for deep device management and policy enforcement. We are committed to transparency regarding how we handle system-level permissions.

## 1. Sensitive Permissions Disclosure
To provide advanced device management features, the app requires several high-level Android permissions:

- **Device Administrator Access:** Used to enforce security policies such as password complexity, screen lock timeouts, and remote data wipe capabilities.
- **Query All Packages:** Required to list all installed applications, allowing administrators to identify and manage (freeze/disable) bloatware or unauthorized software.
- **Hardware Restrictions:** When configured as a Device Owner, the app can disable hardware components like the Camera or Microphone to comply with enterprise security standards.

## 2. Data Privacy & Local Processing
We believe in "Privacy by Design". All device management operations and application metadata are processed locally on the device:
- **Zero External Transmission:** Your list of installed apps, security configurations, and device identifiers are never transmitted to external servers.
- **No Tracking:** The app does not track your location, browsing history, or personal communications.

## 3. User Control & Transparency
You have full control over the app's administrative status:
- You can revoke Device Administrator privileges at any time through system settings.
- The app provides clear logs of all policy changes and application management actions performed.
