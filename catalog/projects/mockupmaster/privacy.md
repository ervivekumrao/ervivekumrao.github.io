# Privacy Policy for Mockup Master

*Last Updated: June 25, 2026*

Welcome to **Mockup Master: Dev Tool**. This application is a specialized utility designed for Android developers and QA engineers to generate mock data.

## 1. Sensitive Permissions Disclosure
- **Permissions:** Uses `WRITE_CONTACTS`, `WRITE_SMS`, and `WRITE_CALL_LOG` to populate mock records into system databases during developer testing.
- **Strict Scope:** Interacts only with data created by the app. Maintains a local registry to enable one-tap surgical cleanup without affecting personal data.

## 2. Local Processing
No server-side components exist. No personal data, device identifiers, or mock entries are ever transmitted externally.
