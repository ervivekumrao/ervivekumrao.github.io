# Privacy Policy for Office Time Counter

*Last Updated: June 17, 2026*

Welcome to **Office Time Counter**. This document outlines how we handle your data, focusing on Location and Notification permissions.

## 1. Location Data Disclosure
- **Geofence Monitoring:** The app accesses Background Location (`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`) to monitor when your device enters or leaves your configured office coordinates.
- **Local Processing:** All location calculations happen locally on your device via the Android Geofencing API.
- **No Server Uploads:** We do not transmit your location or movement history to external servers.

## 2. Notification Access
- Used solely to identify check-in/check-out messages sent by organization-approved companion apps. Personal messages are completely ignored.

## 3. Your Controls
You can revoke permissions at any time or delete your entire attendance history from the app settings.
