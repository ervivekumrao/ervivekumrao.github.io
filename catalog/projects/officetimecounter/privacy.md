# Privacy Policy for Office Time Counter

*Last updated: June 25, 2026*

Office Time Counter uses background geofencing to log office attendance locally on your device.

## 1. Background Location
Location data is used solely to detect when your device enters or leaves your configured office geofence. Location logs are stored locally.

## 2. Data Storage
Your attendance records and weekly targets are stored locally on your device.
